Pulse

A living interface that translates activity and energy into responsive visual signals.

Information with a heartbeat.

Pulse reimagines dense data as a dynamic visual field. The system uses scale, tempo, and contrast to help people recognize patterns at a glance.

Prototype-led design shaped an interaction language that remains expressive while keeping every state legible and useful.
